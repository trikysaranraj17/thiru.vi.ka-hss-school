Since 1955

Our Journey

A timeline of milestones spanning over seven decades of dedication to education.

1955

Foundation Year

Thiru.Vi.Ka. Higher Secondary School was established in Shenoy Nagar, Chennai, with a vision to provide quality education to the community. Named after the great Tamil scholar and freedom fighter Thiru.Vi.Kalyanasundaram.

1960s

Growing Foundations

The school expanded its infrastructure and curriculum, establishing itself as a premier educational institution in North Chennai. New classrooms and science laboratories were built to accommodate growing student enrollment.

1970s

Academic Excellence Begins

The school gained recognition for its outstanding academic results. Students began achieving top ranks in state examinations, establishing a tradition of excellence that continues today.

1980s

Sports & Cultural Growth

Beyond academics, the school developed strong sports and cultural programs. Students excelled in inter-school competitions, winning numerous state-level awards in athletics, cricket, and cultural events.

1990s

Modernization Era

Computer education was introduced, keeping pace with India's IT revolution. The school embraced technology in education, setting up computer labs and incorporating digital learning into the curriculum.

2000s

Golden Jubilee

Celebrated 50 years of educational excellence! The school organized a grand alumni reunion and upgraded its infrastructure with modern classrooms, a new library, and advanced science laboratories.

2010s

Digital Transformation

Smart classrooms and digital learning tools were implemented. The school adopted online assessment systems and interactive teaching methods, preparing students for the digital age.

2020s

Resilience & Innovation

Successfully adapted to online learning during the pandemic and emerged stronger. The school launched its digital portal and continued to maintain its exceptional 98% pass rate.

2026 — Present

70 years & Counting ✨

Today, Thiru.Vi.Ka. HSS stands as a beacon of educational excellence in Chennai, with 30+ dedicated staff members, state-of-the-art facilities, and a 98% pass rate. The legacy continues as we shape the leaders of tomorrow.
